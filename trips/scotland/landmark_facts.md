## 1. St Giles’ Cathedral, the High Kirk

- “The church was founded by King David I in 1124.” [St Giles’ Cathedral history](https://www.stgilescathedral.org.uk/history/)
- “The crown steeple probably dates from around 1500; its exact date is uncertain.” [University of St Andrews, Corpus of Scottish Medieval Parish Churches](https://arts.st-andrews.ac.uk/corpusofscottishchurches/site.php?id=158554)
- “John Knox became St Giles’ minister in 1559 and led its Protestant Reformation.” [St Giles’ Cathedral history](https://www.stgilescathedral.org.uk/history/)
- “The choir ceiling was decorated in 1875–76; surviving painted details are often hard to see.” [Friends of St Giles’ Cathedral](https://www.fosgc.org/st-giles-decoration)
- “Robert Lorimer designed the Thistle Chapel, added in 1910.” [Historic Environment Scotland](https://portal.historicenvironment.scot/apex/f?p=1505%3A300%3A%3A%3A%3A%3AVIEWTYPE%2CVIEWREF%3Adesignation%2CLB27381)
- “The west window is a 1985 Robert Burns memorial by Icelandic artist Leifur Breiðfjörð.” [St Giles’ Cathedral history](https://www.stgilescathedral.org.uk/history/)
- “A surprising detail: St Giles’ was briefly a cathedral in the seventeenth century, but today it is a Presbyterian High Kirk.” [National Records of Scotland](https://catalogue.nrscotland.gov.uk/nrsonlinecatalogue/browseDetails.aspx?reference=CH2%2F136)

## 2. The Falshaw stained-glass window

- “The great east window depicts both the Crucifixion and the Ascension.” [Historic guide to St Giles’ memorial windows](https://www.electricscotland.com/bible/illustratedguide00meikiala.pdf)
- “The guide credits the window to Ballantine of Edinburgh.” [Historic guide to St Giles’ memorial windows](https://www.electricscotland.com/bible/illustratedguide00meikiala.pdf)
- “It is the great east window, at the east end of the choir.” [Historic guide to St Giles’ memorial windows](https://www.electricscotland.com/bible/illustratedguide00meikiala.pdf)
- “The inscription’s 1874–77 dates identify Falshaw’s Lord Provost term.” [Historic guide to St Giles’ memorial windows](https://www.electricscotland.com/bible/illustratedguide00meikiala.pdf)
