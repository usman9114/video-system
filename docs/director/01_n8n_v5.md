## Owner feedback on v5 (2026-11-27), HARD RULES from now on
1. **No cuts while he's talking.** A spoken line stays on his face from first word to last. B-roll, canvas and photo
   cutaways may only sit **between** lines (in a pause or beat), never mid-sentence. Graphics during speech = overlays
   on the face shot only (chips, strip, captions).
2. **Lip-sync in v5 isn't good enough.** Suspects to fix and measure before the next render: punch-in zooms up to 1.22×
   magnify mouth artifacts; three clips used reversed ("bounce") footage; the original footage's mouth movements
   (he was saying other words) may leak through the dub. Next: SyncNet scores per clip vs sync-3, zoom ≤ 1.05 on talking
   shots, no reversed footage.
